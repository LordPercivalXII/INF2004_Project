Buddy 5 Prompt Guide
====================

The current Buddy 5 scope is adaptive servo scanning with an HC-SR04 sensor.
The authoritative implementation and test notes are:

  BUDDY5_IMPLEMENTATION.md
  BUDDY5_TESTING_CHECKLIST.md
  BUDDY5_ALIGNMENT_REVIEW.md
  BUDDY5_IMPLEMENTATION_PROMPT.txt

Frozen hardware pins from the Group 19 Design Review:
  TRIG  = GP17
  ECHO  = GP16 (level-shift to 3.3 V)
  Servo = GP15

The required coarse sweep is -45, 0, and +45 degrees relative to servo center.
When the coarse sweep detects an obstacle, refine that region and publish
distance, width estimate, left/right clearance, and a bypass direction to the
Vehicle Controller. The controller, not Buddy 5, issues motor commands.

Important: older copies of this guide specified GP3/GP7 and 10 Hz single-beam
scanning. Those specifications are obsolete and conflict with the Design
Review and HC-SR04 datasheet.
