Buddy 5 Handoff
===============

Buddy 5 is the ultrasonic obstacle-scanning subsystem. Read
BUDDY5_IMPLEMENTATION.md and BUDDY5_IMPLEMENTATION_PROMPT.txt before changing
the implementation.

Use these project references:
  - INF2004_Group19_Design Review (11).pdf
  - Project Briefing (3).pdf
  - Project Write-up.pdf
  - HCSR04.pdf

The agreed pins are TRIG GP17, ECHO GP16, and servo PWM GP15. GP16 must receive
a 3.3 V-safe ECHO signal through a divider or level shifter. The coarse servo
scan is -45/0/+45 degrees, with a fine scan around detected obstacles.

The subsystem publishes its profile and bypass direction to the Vehicle
Controller. It must not command motors itself. See
BUDDY5_TESTING_CHECKLIST.md for the software and physical checks still needed.
